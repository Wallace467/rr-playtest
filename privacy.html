Range Rat privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Range Rat is made by Wallace Labs Kft., a company registered in Budapest, Hungary. We are responsible for the personal data described here (the "controller"). Contact about your data: privacy@wallacelabs.dev (email). Anything else: hello@wallacelabs.dev (email).

The short version

  • The game doesn't collect personal data about you or send it to us. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your saves stay on your device, and in Steam Cloud if you play on Steam.
  • Only in playtest versions can you choose to send us feedback. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and the form shows you exactly what will be sent.

1. What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and the profile name you type (up to 16 characters). In a browser or phone app this uses the browser's storage; on desktop it uses files in your user folder. This is only so the game works, and it is never sent to us. To delete it, delete the save in the game, clear the site's data in your browser, remove the app, or delete the game's folder.

The desktop version also writes a log file (range-rat.log) to help with troubleshooting. It includes technical details and your Steam display name, and it stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.

2. Steam

If you play on Steam, Valve runs your purchase, your Steam account, achievements, Steam Cloud (which copies your save file to Valve's servers so it follows you between computers) and "rich presence" (Steam friends can see which range you're playing). Valve handles this under Steam's own privacy policy. We don't receive personal data about you from Valve, only sales totals.

3. Playtest feedback (playtest versions only)

If you press Send in the feedback form, we receive:

  • what you type, the tags you pick, and your name if you give one;
  • a screenshot of the game screen;
  • technical details: the game version, your browser or device type (user agent), screen size, language settings, game settings, progress and performance figures, and recent error messages;
  • your save code, only if you tick "Attach my save" (it includes your profile name).

This goes to a private channel on Discord that only we can see. Discord (Discord Inc., USA) handles it under its own privacy policy. Like any website you send something to, Discord also sees your IP address.

Why: to find and fix bugs and improve the game. Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you give by pressing Send. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete your feedback.

How long: we delete feedback once we no longer need it, and at the latest 12 months after the game's release. We delete save codes as soon as we've reproduced the bug.

Playtest feedback is for people aged 16 or over, or with a parent's permission. Prefer not to send anything? Use "Copy report" instead and share it however you like.

4. Websites that host the playtests

Playtest versions are hosted on itch.io and on GitHub Pages. Like most websites, they log visitors' IP addresses under their own privacy policies. We don't receive those logs.

5. If you email us

We use your email address and message to reply, and keep them for up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it elsewhere. Write to privacy@wallacelabs.dev (email). Since we hold almost nothing, it helps if you tell us roughly when you sent feedback and the name you used. You can also complain to the Hungarian data protection authority, the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), www.naih.hu, or to the authority where you live.

7. Children

Range Rat is suitable for all ages. The released game collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

8. Changes

If this changes, for example for a phone app, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
